01 / THE WORKSHOP

Less watching.
More making.

Gareth Brown guides the group through two studio lighting setups, with live demonstrations, model direction, shooting rotations and a tethered image review. The emphasis is on understanding what light does—and learning to use it deliberately.

Morning / Model one

Classic Hollywood.

Inspired by the timeless glamour of Marlene Dietrich, Anna May Wong and Ava Gardner. Dark hair, strong expression, an elegant gown and carefully sculpted light. Explore the drama of classic portraiture through studio flash, LEDs, shadows and subtle coloured accents.

Your challenge / A Hollywood headshot + a glamour portrait
Afternoon / Model two

Colour meets movement.

A spirited, contemporary look inspired by Twiggy, Julie Christie and Agyness Deyn. Experiment with luminous colour, light fabric, reflections, movement and the balance between flash and continuous LED light.

Your challenge / A colour portrait + an experimental image

02 / HOW THE DAY WORKS

A studio you can learn in.

Digital tethered shooting means everyone can see each lighting decision on a shared screen. You will work in guided rotations rather than competing for camera time.

When it is not your turn to shoot, there is still plenty to learn: observe the light, anticipate an adjustment, compare frames and prepare a new idea.

THE EXPERIENCE

  • Guided shooting rotations in a working studio
